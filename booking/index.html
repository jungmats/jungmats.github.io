Why AI agents matter for your site

AI agents — ChatGPT, Perplexity, shopping and research assistants — are increasingly browsing, reading, and acting on websites on behalf of their users. Most sites weren't built with that in mind: content, structure, and business models still assume a human is on the other end.

That raises two questions worth answering:

  • Is your site actually usable by AI agents today?
  • Is there a way to turn that traffic into an opportunity, rather than letting it pass through unnoticed?

If either question is on your mind, the most useful next step is a short call. We'll look at your specific situation together, and you'll walk away knowing whether this is worth pursuing — and if so, what a first concrete step would look like.
