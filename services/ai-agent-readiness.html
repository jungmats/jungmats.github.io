AI Agent Readiness Audit & Optimization

Make your website ready for an AI-agent-driven customer journey.

We identify the customer intents that matter, test how effectively AI agents understand and act on your website, and recommend or implement targeted improvements — from clearer content and structured data to customized agent-facing experiences.

The Problem

More customers are using AI assistants and agents to research companies, compare offers, evaluate products and services, and decide what to do next.

A website can work perfectly well for a human visitor while still being difficult for an AI agent to interpret reliably. Important information may be ambiguous, spread across multiple pages, hidden behind complex interfaces, or simply missing.

The result: an AI agent may misunderstand your offer, fail to determine whether you are a good fit, overlook important proof points, or recommend a competitor whose information is easier to interpret.

The question is no longer only “Does my website work for humans and search engines?” It is increasingly also: “Can AI agents correctly understand, evaluate, and interact with my business?”

How We Help

Identify what matters. We define the customer journeys and questions that matter most to your business — from discovering your company to evaluating an offer or taking the next step.

Test the current experience. We test how AI assistants and agents handle those real customer intents — combining automated testing across leading AI engines with hands-on retrieval checks — and identify where they succeed, fail, or produce incomplete or inaccurate results. Where useful, we also give you a short set of prompts to try yourself in the consumer AI apps your own customers use.

Find the gaps. We determine why those failures occur — whether the issue is content, information architecture, technical accessibility, structured data, missing information, or the way important actions are exposed.

Improve the highest-value areas. We recommend the smallest effective changes first. Depending on the problem, this may mean improving existing pages, restructuring information, adding machine-readable data, or creating a dedicated agent-facing experience.

Validate the improvement. Where changes are implemented, the original scenarios are tested again to measure whether agent performance has actually improved — immediately for what your website directly controls, and again a few weeks later for results that depend on AI search indexes catching up.

Who It’s For

This engagement is particularly relevant for companies whose website plays an important role in:

  • Customer discovery and lead generation
  • Product or service evaluation
  • Customer qualification
  • Booking and reservations
  • Quote and inquiry requests

Typical clients include organizations where the website is a critical part of the customer journey, but there is no dedicated product, AI, or web platform team responsible for making that journey work effectively for AI agents.

What You Get

The engagement helps you answer three practical questions:

  • Can AI agents correctly understand and use our website for the customer journeys that matter?
  • Where are they failing — and why?
  • Which changes are worth making now, and which are not?

Typical outcomes include:

  • A clear view of how AI agents currently interpret your business
  • Identification of important customer intents that are not being served reliably
  • Concrete examples of agent failures and missed opportunities
  • A prioritized set of improvements based on business impact
  • Recommendations covering content, website structure, structured data, and technical architecture
  • Where relevant, a way for agents to complete actions on your customers' behalf — not just read about them
  • Optional implementation of selected improvements
  • Before/after validation to measure the effect

Common Questions

How much of my own time do I need to invest?

A 60–90 minute kick-off workshop to define your priority customer intents, plus a review and decision step once findings are ready — either a 60–90 minute session or, for fast-moving teams, a short round of decisions as findings arrive. Also: approval of any new or restructured content before it goes live. Testing and analysis run independently.

What is required from my side?

A business or marketing stakeholder who knows your customers and website objectives, and a technical contact where needed. No production credentials are required for the audit; implementation packages may need access to your CMS, repository, or hosting.

What phases and deliverables should I expect?

Five phases: intent workshop, baseline agent testing and diagnosis, recommendations and optimization blueprint, a review and decision step, and optional pilot implementation with before/after validation. Deliverables include an agent intent map, an agent readiness scorecard with raw test evidence, a prioritized recommendation backlog, and a before/after validation report (delivered in two parts, since visibility results take longer to confirm than content results) for implementation work.

How long do the results last, and what follow-up is needed?

AI platforms and websites evolve, so agent readiness is a state to maintain, not a one-time certificate. The same intent test suite can be re-run periodically or after significant changes to catch regressions and new gaps.

Is this SEO?

No. Success is measured against actual agent task completion on real customer intents — whether agents can understand, evaluate, and act on the business — not against search rankings. The approach is vendor-neutral.

Does this require rebuilding our website?

No. We work within your existing platform — WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, a headless CMS, or a custom-built site. Content and structural optimizations are built directly into your site. If the project includes agent actions, they typically run through a small service connected to your platform. Either way, no rebuild is needed.
