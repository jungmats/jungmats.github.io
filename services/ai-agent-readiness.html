AI Agent Readiness Audit & Optimization

Make your website ready for an AI-agent-driven customer journey.

I identify the customer intents that matter, test how effectively AI agents understand and act on your website, and recommend or implement targeted improvements — from clearer content and structured data to customized agent-facing experiences.

The Problem

More customers are using AI assistants and agents to research companies, compare offers, evaluate products and services, and decide what to do next.

A website can work perfectly well for a human visitor while still being difficult for an AI agent to interpret reliably. Important information may be ambiguous, spread across multiple pages, hidden behind complex interfaces, or simply missing.

The result: an AI agent may misunderstand your offer, fail to determine whether you are a good fit, overlook important proof points, or recommend a competitor whose information is easier to interpret.

The question is no longer only “Does my website work for humans and search engines?” It is increasingly also: “Can AI agents correctly understand, evaluate, and interact with my business?”

How I Help

Identify what matters. We define the customer journeys and questions that matter most to your business — from discovering your company to evaluating an offer or taking the next step.

Test the current experience. I test how relevant AI assistants and agents handle those real customer intents and identify where they succeed, fail, or produce incomplete or inaccurate results.

Find the gaps. I determine why those failures occur — whether the issue is content, information architecture, technical accessibility, structured data, missing information, or the way important actions are exposed.

Improve the highest-value areas. I recommend the smallest effective changes first. Depending on the problem, this may mean improving existing pages, restructuring information, adding machine-readable data, or creating a dedicated agent-facing experience.

Validate the improvement. Where changes are implemented, the original scenarios can be tested again to measure whether agent performance has actually improved.

What You Get

The engagement helps you answer three practical questions:

  • Can AI agents correctly understand and use our website for the customer journeys that matter?
  • Where are they failing — and why?
  • Which changes are worth making now, and which are not?

Typical outcomes include:

  • A clear view of how AI agents currently interpret your business
  • Identification of important customer intents that are not being served reliably
  • Concrete examples of agent failures and missed opportunities
  • A prioritized set of improvements based on business impact
  • Recommendations covering content, website structure, structured data, and technical architecture
  • Optional implementation of selected improvements
  • Before/after validation to measure the effect
